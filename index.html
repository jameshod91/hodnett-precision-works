HOW IT WORKS

From your idea to the finished piece.

A simple four-step process keeps your custom CNC or laser project clear from the first idea through the final piece.

01

Tell Us Your Idea

Send us what you have in mind. A sketch, photo, logo, dimensions, or even just an idea is enough to get started.

02

We Design It

We work out the details and create a design for your CNC or laser project. You can approve it before production begins.

03

We Make It

Once approved, your project is CNC carved, cut, or laser engraved with attention to detail and craftsmanship.

04

It's Ready

We finish your piece and arrange shipping or local pickup when available, so it gets to you ready to enjoy.
